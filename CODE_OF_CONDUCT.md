# Community conduct

Keep discussions useful, respectful, and focused on the project. Welcome questions and
constructive disagreement. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, publish private information,
or share credentials and private media links.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, restrict interaction, or close discussions
that violate these expectations. Reports about conduct can be sent privately to the maintainer
at majoros.lukas@pm.me. Reports are handled privately with the people needed to resolve them.
